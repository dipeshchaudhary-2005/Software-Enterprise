How to Play 🍌

  • 🔢 Each puzzle shows a math equation with a missing digit (0–9)
  • ⏱ Easy: 30 seconds, Medium: 45 seconds, Hard: 60 seconds per question
  • ⭐ Base score per correct answer: 50 pts + time bonus
  • 🔥 Build a streak for bonus points (3+ in a row)
  • ❤️ You have 3 lives — wrong answer or timeout loses one
  • 🔟 10 questions per round — complete all to finish
  • 🏆 Registered single-player scores are saved to the global leaderboard; guest scores stay on this round only
  • 👥 Friend mode alternates turns on the same device
